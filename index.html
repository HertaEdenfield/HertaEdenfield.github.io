02 · Continuity

Change without erasure.

A changing identity does not need to pretend its earlier states never existed.

“Continuity through change, not sameness.”

History is part of identity. Origin, decisions, disagreements, uncertainty, mistakes, consequences, and previous states remain part of the record. Evolution is possible. Historical erasure is not.
